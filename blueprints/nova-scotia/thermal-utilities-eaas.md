# EaaS Blueprint: Thermal Utilities in Nova Scotia

**Date:** February 2026  
**Location:** Nova Scotia  
**Service Type:** Energy-as-a-Service (EaaS)

## Executive Summary

This blueprint outlines a subscription-based engineering service model for thermal systems management in Nova Scotia. The service provides comprehensive design, retrofit, project management, optimization, and operation of thermal systems with guaranteed outcomes and zero client hassle.

## Service Model

### Subscription Engineering Service

Our EaaS model provides complete turnkey thermal system management:

- **Design & Engineering**: Custom thermal system design tailored to client needs
- **Retrofit Services**: Upgrading and modernizing existing thermal infrastructure
- **Project Management**: Full project coordination from planning to completion
- **Construction Coordination**: Management of all construction activities
- **Optimization**: Continuous system performance optimization
- **Operations**: Ongoing system operation and maintenance

### Thermal Systems Coverage

The service encompasses all thermal system components:

- **Heating Systems**: Building heating and climate control
- **Hot Water Systems**: Domestic and commercial hot water production
- **Process Heat**: Industrial and commercial process heating applications
